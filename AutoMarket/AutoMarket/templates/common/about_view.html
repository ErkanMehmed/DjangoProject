About AutoMarket

AutoMarket is a small vehicle marketplace built with Django. It lets you keep a catalog of manufacturers, list vehicles for sale, and search through them.

What you can do

Manage manufacturers

Add, view, edit and delete car brands, with their country, founding year, description and logo.

List vehicles

Create listings with price, mileage, fuel type, transmission, condition and photos.

Pick equipment features

Attach any number of features, such as heated seats or parking sensors, to each vehicle.

Search and filter

Find vehicles by model name, manufacturer and maximum price, then sort the results.

How the data fits together

  • Manufacturer → has many vehicles. A manufacturer that still has vehicles cannot be deleted.
  • Vehicle ↔ has many features, and a feature can belong to many vehicles.
